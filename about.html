My Story

The developer behind the websites, stores, designs and ads.

I'm Rao Abdur Rehman, a web developer and e-commerce specialist based in Sargodha, Pakistan, working with clients worldwide. I build every type of website — from business sites and online stores to high-premium, cinematic CGI-style experiences.

My work covers the full journey of an online business: a website that looks premium, a Shopify store that sells, design that makes the brand look established, and Meta ads that bring the right customers in. Clients in the US have trusted me with their company websites, and my store work has generated real sales within days of launch.

Alongside client work, I'm studying Data Science at the University of Sargodha — so everything I build is backed by an eye for numbers, not just design.

I work remote only, with fast replies on WhatsApp and clear, simple communication from the first message to launch day.
